Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Spendly is built to help you track your personal expenses with as little friction as possible. This policy explains what data we collect, why we need it, and what we do — and do not do — with it.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the service, we collect the information you choose to provide when you create an account and use Spendly. This includes:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Expense records you create, including amount, category, date, and any description you add.
  • Basic usage data, such as the pages you visit and the actions you take, to help us improve the product.

We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, card numbers, or transaction records from external institutions. Spendly is a manual tracking tool — everything you see in the app was entered by you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect strictly to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Store and display your expense records across your sessions and devices.
  • Generate the category breakdowns, monthly summaries, and trend views you see in the app.
  • Diagnose performance issues and understand which features are most useful.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your expense records to build advertising profiles, and we do not share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by trusted infrastructure providers. Access to your account is protected by your password, and you are encouraged to choose a strong password and keep it confidential. Even though we work hard to protect your information, no system is perfectly secure — please notify us immediately if you believe your account has been compromised.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time from your profile settings. Once deleted, your records are removed from our active systems, though limited residual copies may persist in backups for a short period as part of standard data-protection procedures.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third-party providers to operate the service — for example, hosting infrastructure and outbound email delivery. These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their function and are contractually obligated to protect it.

We do not embed advertising trackers or analytics that follow you across other websites. If we ever introduce optional third-party integrations, we will update this policy and request your consent before activating them.

5. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone in that age group. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you through the service. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on our website. We will do our best to respond to your request in a timely and transparent manner.
